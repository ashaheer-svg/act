Minimizing Report Methodology to a Button by Default

Currently across all business reports (Commercial Invoices, Unpaid Invoices, Warranties, LTV, Churn, Tax Audit, EOL, Contracts, etc.), the .methodology-card header is rendered full-width directly below the command bar. Even when collapsed, it consumes 78px of vertical screen real estate, pushing down the critical KPI summary ribbon, charts, and invoice data tables.
