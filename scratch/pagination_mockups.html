Architectural Comparison of Pagination Designs

Recommended Choice
Concept B: Modern Floating Rail
Individual 28px square icon buttons paired with numerical page pills. Provides highest visual affordance, distinct hit targets, and clear active state.
  • Minimized Lucide Icons: Dedicated First (««), Prev (‹), Next (›), and Last (»») icons.
  • 28px Compact Height: Perfect fit for high-density enterprise tables without blowing up vertical space.
  • Intelligent Number Truncation: Shows 1 ... 14 [15] 16 ... 497 so wide page sets never overflow.
  • Integrated Direct Jump: Type page number and hit Enter to jump instantly across 10,000+ records.
  • Universal Application: Cleanly drops into Invoices, EOL, Churn, LTV, Tax Audit, and Unpaid Debtors.
Concept A: Segmented Micro-Dock
All controls fused into a single continuous border pill. Extremely compact horizontal profile, zero gaps between buttons.
  • Zero Margin Waste: Buttons share common interior divider borders.
  • Clean Sleek Boundary: Enclosed in a single 1px Slate-300 perimeter.
  • Minimized Icon Set: Same chevron glyphs embedded seamlessly.
  • Slightly smaller individual tap targets than Concept B.
Concept C: Ultra-Dense Micro Pager
Minimalist toolbar layout featuring ghost icon chevrons surrounding an editable inline page input pill.
  • Smallest Footprint: Under 250px total width.
  • Ideal for Narrow Panels: Perfect for the split drawer inspector or mobile screens.
  • Direct Inline Editing: Click the page number directly to edit and press enter.
  • No direct sibling page number buttons (1, 2, 3) to click sequentially.
